MARKET ANALYSIS
Quantify the revenue opportunity
•Total Addressable Market (TAM)
= Total market demand
•Serviceable Available Market (SAM)
= % of TAM that you can target
•Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM)
= % of SAM that can be realistically obtained

MARKET ANALYSIS
Ex: Fast Food Burger Stand
•TAM = Worldwide fast food market
•SAM = % of TAM that considers factors like location,
population, fast food habits, fast food revenues
•SOM = % of SAM within reach (considers
competitors, location, etc.)
